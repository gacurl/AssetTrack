# AssetTrack Demo Script (2 Minutes)

## Goal
Show that AssetTrack is:
- simple to use
- reliable
- built for real-world asset tracking

---

## 1. Open (10 seconds)

**Say**
> “AssetTrack answers one question:  
> *Where is each asset, and who has it right now?*”

**Do**
- Show the dashboard

---

## 2. Issue Workflow (45 seconds)

**Say**
> “Issuing an asset is a controlled process—nothing happens until we confirm it.”

**Do**
1. Click **Issue**
2. Select a holder
3. Set building + room
4. Click **Save current location**
5. Scan (or simulate) an asset

**Say**
> “Assets are staged first. This prevents mistakes.”

**Do**
6. Click **Preview / Confirm**

**Say**
> “We review before committing.”

**Do**
7. Check both confirmations
8. Click **Commit Issue**

**Say**
> “Now it’s recorded. We have a full audit trail and a receipt.”

---

## 3. Receipt (20 seconds)

**Do**
- Open the receipt

**Say**
> “Every action creates a receipt.  
> It shows what happened, who did it, and where.”

**Do**
- Click **Download PDF**

**Say**
> “We can also send this as an email record.”

---

## 4. Return Workflow (30 seconds)

**Say**
> “Returns follow the same pattern—stage, review, commit.”

**Do**
1. Click **Return**
2. Scan the same asset
3. Click **Preview / Confirm**
4. Confirm checkboxes
5. Click **Commit Return**

**Say**
> “Now the asset is back in storage.”

---

## 5. Admin + Security (30 seconds)

**Say**
> “Admin controls are simple but strict.”

**Do**
- Open **Users**

**Say**
> “We can disable users, change roles, or reset passwords.”

**Do**
- Show **Generate Temp Password**

**Say**
> “Password reset is secure:
> - temporary password
> - shown once
> - user must change it before doing anything”

**Point to status**

> “And if a user is disabled, they still cannot log in—even with a temp password.”

---

## 6. Close (15 seconds)

**Say**
> “AssetTrack is designed for real conditions:
> - clear workflows
> - no guessing
> - full accountability”

> “Scan → Review → Commit  
> That’s the system.”

---

## Optional (if time allows)

- Show pointer cursor behavior (quick UX polish)
- Mention:
  > “Runs offline-first with a full audit history”

---

## Backup Talking Points (if asked)

- “What prevents mistakes?”  
  → Preview before commit

- “Can records be changed?”  
  → No, events are append-only

- “What if someone forgets their password?”  
  → Admin reset with forced change

- “How do you prove what happened?”  
  → Receipts + audit history

---

## Timing Summary

- Intro: 10s  
- Issue: 45s  
- Receipt: 20s  
- Return: 30s  
- Admin: 30s  
- Close: 15s  

**Total: ~2 minutes**
