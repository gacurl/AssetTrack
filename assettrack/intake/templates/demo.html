The problem

Equipment tracking gets messy.

Equipment tracking gets messy when records live in memory, spreadsheets, emails, and notes.

Teams need a simple way to answer:

  • Who has this asset?
  • Where should it be?
  • When did it last move?
  • What proof do we have?
  • Where is the documentation?

How AssetTrack helps

Add, issue, return, and keep proof.

AssetTrack helps your team add, issue, and return assets while keeping proof for each move.

Email keeps your customer informed and helps build trust and accountability.

Email does not move assets. Saved Issue and Return actions create the proof.

Works where the team works

Local or hosted.

AssetTrack can run on a local computer, a local server, or a hosted server.

It is built for offline-first use, so teams are not stuck when internet access is limited.

Start with a spreadsheet

Bring current records forward.

AssetTrack can load your current asset records from a spreadsheet.

After setup, the team can use the app to add, issue, or return assets, while keeping proof in one place.

How AssetTrack fixes the pain

Simple tools, clear roles.

  • Your team gets simple tools to issue assets, return assets, find records, and read receipts.
  • Admins control users, locations, backups, recovery, and system settings.
